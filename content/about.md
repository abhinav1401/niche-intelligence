# About This Application

**Niche Intelligence — reviewer information**

Niche Intelligence is a private research application being prepared for review of its proposed YouTube API analytics and derived-metrics use case. The public demonstration reuses its actual dashboard and precomputed synthetic examples. It is not the full live system, and no acceptance or approval is claimed.

## Purpose and independent value

The application helps an independent creator investigate subjects for original, curiosity-driven 3D-animated Shorts: hidden mechanisms, science scenarios, engineering, biology, historical incidents and adjacent factual questions. It supports validation experiments and reusable production planning; it does not generate videos, upload to YouTube, copy scripts, or guarantee virality.

## Why the private tool uses YouTube Data API v3

The API supplies public channel and video metadata and available statistics through supported collection methods rather than scraping. Search results identify relevant videos and their creators, including non-3D channels. Uploads playlists and batched video lookups expand evidence efficiently. The tool can process channel/video identifiers, URLs, titles, descriptions, public subscriber/view/like/comment counts where available, publication times, duration, categories and thumbnail links. It does not collect comment text or video files. No visitor account authorisation, private channel access or YouTube playback is offered by this demonstration.

## Autonomous discovery

The private workflow starts across twelve broad subject families, reuses previous results, expands toward novel adjacent questions and stops when evidence, novelty or quota limits justify stopping. Selection seeks subject and creator-size diversity, includes smaller creators, respects exclusions and treats common ownership as provisional. Search-ranked coverage is a biased sample, not a complete market census. The three initial reference creators are optional seeds rather than the entire market.

## Proposed custom analytics, subject to policy permission

The analytics pipeline groups videos by specific subjects plus curiosity mechanism, storytelling format and visual advantage. Channel-relative median comparisons, age cohorts, robust statistics and observed snapshots help distinguish repeatable outliers from one-off totals. Historical growth is calculated only from actual stored observations in a permitted live workflow; no historical counts are inferred from a current total. Shorts status remains uncertain because duration alone does not confirm placement.

Demand combines repeated performance and independent-creator evidence with disclosed supporting signals. Competition describes sampled supply, concentration and recency; explanation quality requires human-reviewed evidence. Five editable heuristic factors initially weight demand 30%, underserved supply 25%, 3D advantage 20%, repeatability 15% and momentum 10%, with confidence adjustment. The scores and production priors are this application's proposed independent metrics, never substitutes for YouTube's published counts. They help connect evidence to pilot concepts and reusable 3D assets. They are not calibrated forecasts or proof of an empty market.

## Freshness, retention and quota protection

The private tool keeps separate channel/video tables, observation snapshots, provenance, run state and accounting in local SQLite. Collection timestamps and unavailable values remain visible. Default non-authorised public-statistics retention is 30 days, with metadata refresh/deletion and cache expiry handled by maintenance. Extended statistical retention requires actual applicable permission and is capped at 36 calendar months; the demonstration does not assert that such permission exists. Exports and backups require operator handling too.

Before each live attempt the private collector atomically reserves every relevant daily/minute budget in SQLite. Failed attempts, retries and pages remain accounted for. Daily limits use Pacific midnight and daylight-saving rules. Quota exhaustion suspends work with saved checkpoints and explicit resume. Local limits cannot see another application's project usage. Optional provider reconciliation requires separate authorisation; it is not connected here.

## What reviewers can explore here

All eight original research pages, charts, evidence calculations, topic detail, example episode briefs, a Research Control Centre walkthrough, quota-monitor design and read-only Settings tabs are available. The bundled dataset contains 24 fictional creators and 1,920 synthetic videos at a fixed date. The research example and quotas were produced offline before publication. Filters are temporary; no visitor changes reach the dataset. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms from the always-visible sidebar links.

This distribution omits live collector/worker implementations, credentials, configuration from the private tool and real research databases. It cannot switch to Live mode, execute research, reserve quota, call model providers, import files, refresh data or save annotations. The SQLite fixture is opened read-only and only precomputed results are displayed. Host-level web delivery and logs are separate from research activity.

## Features awaiting applicable acceptance

Live derived outlier scores, semantic classification, demand/competition estimates, opportunity rankings and evidence-backed episode recommendations remain protected by the private application's existing actual-approval setting. Public collection evidence can be gathered privately within quota, but no live derived recommendations are represented as permitted merely because this demo exists. The frozen synthetic analytics illustrate the proposed behavior for review. No publicly launched subscription service exists.
