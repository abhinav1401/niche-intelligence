The policy drafts are based on the audited implementation and official YouTube/Google/Streamlit material reviewed 31 October 2026. They are not a legal opinion or compliance certification.

The owner approved Abhinav Chaudhary and steering9954@gmail.com on 31 October 2026; these public details are now in `site.json`. Do not use a private Cloud-account email, home address or credentials.

The deployed Privacy Policy discloses hosting usage/viewership analytics, third-party telemetry and operational logging; do not describe the Cloud deployment as telemetry-free. See the official Community Cloud status/limitations documentation.
